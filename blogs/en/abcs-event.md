**A Journey of Growth and Community Building**<br/><br/>

When I first came to Purdue University, I never imagined that I would become a Teaching Assistant for one of my favorite classes, Computer Architecture (CS250). Now, I realize how much I love helping and guiding students to success, and I plan to continue teaching. This passion for supporting others also inspired me to take things a bit further.

This year, alongside my classmate Danielle E, we founded the Association of Black Computer Scientists (ABCS) at Purdue. Our mission is to foster professional and cultural development, scholarship, and community welfare. ABCS is mainly focused on creating space for Black students within the Computer Science department and those interested in the field. We collaborate with faculty, staff, administration, alums, other student groups, and the broader Purdue community.

Today, we held our second meeting, during which Michael McDunn, a Southwest Airlines Campus Ambassador and Purdue Student, gave a fantastic talk on internship opportunities at Southwest Airlines. It was inspiring to see so many people come together, and I'm excited to see our community continue to grow. Boiler up! 😄
